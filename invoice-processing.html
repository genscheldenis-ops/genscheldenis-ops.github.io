AI-Powered Invoice Processing & Validation

A Make workflow combining Gemini extraction, data validation and duplicate checks to route invoice information across Gmail, Drive and Sheets.

Workflow overview

Incoming invoice messages are handled in Make. Gemini extracts structured information, the workflow checks the extracted data, and a Make Data Store supports duplicate detection before routing records to Google Drive and Google Sheets.

01 / INTAKEGmail
02 / EXTRACTMake + Gemini
03 / CHECKValidate fields
04 / DEDUPEMake Data Store
05 / ROUTEDrive + Sheets

What it covers

  • Invoice intake through Gmail and orchestration in Make.
  • AI-assisted extraction of invoice information with Gemini.
  • Validation before information moves to the next step.
  • Duplicate checking with a Make Data Store.
  • Routing to Google Drive and Google Sheets.

Design priorities

Keep each stage understandable, validate extracted information before it is used downstream, and check for duplicates before adding another record. The public description does not claim accounting or tax review.

Any invoice data used in demonstrations should be fictional or fully anonymized.
